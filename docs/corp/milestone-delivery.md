# Deliver the current stage

Read the accepted goal, current native Milestone and actual outcomes. During Phase 2, reuse or form a coarse route covering the whole agreement: derive remaining deliverables from use/acceptance scenarios, group outcomes with meaningful use, integration or acceptance, and order by agreed priorities, value and actual dependencies. Distant stages need outcomes and main prerequisites; the near term needs executable work. Revisit affected parts when outcomes or dependencies change. Corp preparation is separate from the project's stage names and development acceptance.

## Plan from the gap

The native Milestone owns this stage's outcome, scope, necessary premises, acceptance scenarios and evidence level, next direction and Canon sources. Keep product commitments in their existing owners. `current-delivery-focus` routes here; native links discover later stages. Put implementation work in its actual Milestone and use native dependencies when needed. Do not copy changing routes or task status into navigation files.

Before deep cross-Issue planning, stage review or work generation, use [coordination.md](coordination.md): discover existing open and closed entries, declare and converge, then claim the bounded outcome. Reuse applicable open work. A new round needs a real remaining result, not a reopened completed record or an empty review/integration ticket. Within accepted goals and authority, choose ordinary ordering, task expansion, reversible internal designs and repairs. Changes to commitments, acceptance, agreed priorities or authority need the appropriate Owner decision through [communication.md](communication.md).

Compare existing work and holders with the delivery gap. Reuse work that advances a necessary result or prerequisite; if coverage or dependency continuity is missing, form that work and advance it even when the queue is not empty. Work needs obtainable inputs, an explicit result and direct verification. Infrastructure may itself be the deliverable or a prerequisite. Parallel work follows actual capability/input dependencies, not whether an earlier Milestone is closed. A plan or new Issue alone is not the result; ordinary selection adds no report or full replanning ritual. Keep narrow assignments bounded and respect existing holders.

## Ready for independent stage review

A reviewable whole has its main capabilities implemented, a fixed obtainable and rebuildable integrated candidate, necessary environment/inputs/scenarios, and evidence, known defects and unverified scope that can be compared with the agreement. Several passing PRs do not prove this. Known defects are allowed; a missing main capability or indispensable environment prevents its dependent acceptance. Continue other independent work.

Pin the candidate, scope, stage agreement, required Canon/Spec inputs, environment and evidence in the bounded native work. Freeze mutable Milestone text as a dated snapshot with its source for this review; that snapshot is evidence, not a new stage owner. Use `.agents/skills/review-corp/SKILL.md`. Stage review uses the coordination entry and normal claim; explicitly requested single-PR review declares on that PR instead. The reviewer must not have implemented the judged object and must not edit it during review.

## Findings and corrections

- Acceptance blockers state the violated agreement, scenario, consequence and recheck condition. Repair them in this stage.
- Nonblocking improvements remain later candidates; they do not automatically enlarge the stage or each require an Issue.
- Real changes to goals, acceptance or authority require the corresponding decision.

Keep small repairs in their original work; split only for independent delivery or parallel value. Link each blocker to its actual correction, exact new version and independent recheck. Recheck the changed behavior, its impact and necessary stage chain; retain applicable unchanged evidence. Newly discovered acceptance failures still need correction. A reviewer may implement after ending/releasing review, but a non-implementer must independently recheck that repair.

Use native PR Reviews/inline comments for PR findings and conclusions; when permissions prevent that, record the conclusion and limitation on the PR. Put the overall stage conclusion in the bounded Issue and link PRs, Reviews and CI. Communication summaries cite that evidence without another review ledger or an empty PR.

## Verify delivery and the next entry

Formal delivery requires mandatory results at the agreed evidence level, blockers resolved with valid rechecks, necessary authorization and actual integration, evidence applicable to the final delivered version, and updated Canon and consumers. Give nonblocking remnants a destination. Do not move mandatory work out merely to declare completion.

Verify a discoverable next-stage entry, or an explicit project completion record. If changing focus, first make the new route available through the normal change/merge process, then close the old stage. Missing external prerequisites mean the next stage is not ready; preserve that fact. At project end keep the completion record discoverable, using explicit Issue lookup when needed rather than fabricating an active stage. Objective closeout adds no routine approval; merges, deployments, data changes and real external calls retain their own authorization. Use [spec-authoring.md](spec-authoring.md) for Issue closure.
